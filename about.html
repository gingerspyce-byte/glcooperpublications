Meet the author

GingerCooper

Writing as G.L. Cooper

Ginger Cooper is a Canadian author who writes nonfiction as well as children's books and puzzles. Her nonfiction work is driven by subjects that spark curiosity and invite readers to look more closely, learn something new and come away with a deeper understanding of the topic.

Her catalogue is intentionally varied, but the common thread is simple: Ginger wants her books to give readers something worthwhile — useful information, fascinating stories, practical ideas or an enjoyable way to spend time.

Alongside nonfiction, she creates children's books and puzzle books, bringing the same sense of curiosity and care to readers of every age.

The publishing house

About GLCooper Publications

GLCooper Publications is Ginger's independent publishing imprint. It brings together her nonfiction and her wider collection of children's books, puzzles and other reader-focused projects.

Three new nonfiction titles — Behind the Grid, Serial Killers and The Dark Tetrad — are in final proofreading and will be published soon, with Wired Minds and The Trillion-Dollar Ghost following later in 2026.

The puzzle list has an imprint of its own, G.C. Puzzle Books, devoted to large-print word searches, trivia and colouring for adults.
